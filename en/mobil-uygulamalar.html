01 — IDEA AND PLANNING

First, we map the user's journey.

We clarify who the app is for and what problem it solves, and plan the user flow between screens together.

  • Target user and needs analysis
  • Screen flow and feature list
  • Roadmap and release plan
02 — INTERFACE DESIGN

Convenience at your fingertips.

We design readable mobile interfaces that are easy to use one-handed and reflect your brand.

  • Mobile-first UI/UX

03 — DEVELOPMENT

One codebase, two platforms.

We build your app for iOS and Android from a single codebase and integrate it with your server, payments and other systems.

  • iOS and Android together
  • API and system integration
  • Secure sessions and data
04 — TESTING

Real devices, real scenarios.

We test tapping, scrolling and notification scenarios one by one across different screen sizes.

  • Testing across devices and screens
  • Push notification testing
  • Performance and battery usage
05 — STORE LAUNCH AND SUPPORT

In the store, in your pocket.

We publish your app on the App Store and Google Play, and stay by your side with updates and technical support.

  • Store submission and release
  • Regular updates
  • 24/7 technical support
